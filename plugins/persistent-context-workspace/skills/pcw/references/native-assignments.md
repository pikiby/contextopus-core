# Native assignments

Read when delegating real-source investigation or execution. The primary retains task control and authors durable knowledge; no Controller or separate knowledge-search agent is introduced.

Use a fresh native context with `fork_turns: "none"` and the minimum relevant information. Give the subagent:

- One concrete question or implementation outcome, the exact authorized roots or objects, constraints, and explicit non-goals.
- The needed facts from knowledge and their material IDs; include source coordinates when available.
- For edits, exclusive owned targets and the reminder that others share the workspace: preserve their changes and accommodate concurrent work.
- A compact requested result: findings or changes, supporting coordinates, relevant uncertainty or blockers, and corrections to the supplied knowledge IDs.

The worker investigates real files, code, documents, logs, or allowed services within the assignment. Read-only assignments do not authorize changes. Existing execution authority persists; discovery does not add unrelated outcomes. The primary does not repeat the worker's full source inspection.

When reality differs from knowledge, the worker establishes the cause inside its scope and continues if the correction preserves the authorized outcome. A failed attempt alone does not prove memory is stale. If proceeding requires a new choice or authority, it returns the established facts and exact blocker. It also returns useful findings from incomplete work; hypotheses stay hypotheses.

The primary updates affected knowledge from established corrections and decides subsequent work. A content correction does not require a semantic review of every neighbor's links. Knowledge is accumulated evidence, not a guarantee that the live environment is unchanged.
